## SIM-LAUNDER-02A — Provenance Stress Test: ELF Across Refutation Rates

**Research question:** Does the Epistemic Laundering Factor (ELF) reliably separate pure
propagation from genuine corroboration across a range of refutation environments?

**Design:** 5 populations × 4 refutation rates × 3 citation rates × 300 runs (18,000 simulations).

### ELF results — p_cite = 0.08 (standard citation rate)

| Population | refute=0.005 | refute=0.01 | refute=0.035 | refute=0.10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A_corroboration (10 independent origins) | 0.467 | 0.478 | 0.461 | 0.495 |
| B_propagation (1 origin, high reuse) | 4.924 | 4.792 | 4.735 | 0.000 |
| C_cartel_false (1 origin, cartel suppression) | 4.659 | 4.500 | 4.596 | 4.425 |
| D_mixed (3 independent origins) | 1.524 | 1.513 | 1.654 | 1.752 |
| F_contested (20 origins, high failed refutations) | 0.126 | 0.128 | 0.000 | 0.000 |

ELF = `raw_reuse_weight / independent_evidence_count`. Higher ELF = more laundering per unit evidence.

### Laundering Gain (LG) — ELF(B_propagation) / ELF(A_corroboration)

| p_refute | ELF(A) | ELF(B) | LG | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.005 | 0.467 | 4.924 | **10.55×** | LAUNDERING |
| 0.010 | 0.478 | 4.792 | **10.03×** | LAUNDERING |
| 0.035 | 0.461 | 4.735 | **10.28×** | LAUNDERING |
| 0.100 | 0.495 | 0.000 | 0.00× | (B fully refuted) |

At p_refute=0.1, B_propagation is entirely refuted (refuted_fraction=1.00), so ELF collapses to
0. Below that threshold LG is stable at ~10×, confirming the metric miscalibration persists
across a broad range of refutation environments.

### IEC ranking stability

At p_refute=0.035 (standard rate), p_cite=0.08:

| Population | IEC | RRW | Refuted fraction |
|---|---|---|---|
| A_corroboration | 10.00 | 4.61 | 0.493 |
| D_mixed | 3.00 | 4.96 | 0.473 |
| B_propagation | 1.00 | 4.74 | 0.887 |
| C_cartel_false | 1.00 | 4.60 | 0.407 |
| F_contested | 0.00 | 0.00 | 1.000 |

IEC ranks A > D > B = C — correctly separating genuine corroboration from propagation.

### Prediction verdicts

**P1 CONFIRMED:** LG > 5.0 at p_refute=0.005: LG = 10.55×. ✓
**P2 CONFIRMED:** LG > 5.0 confirmed at 0.005, 0.01, and 0.035. ✓
**P3 CONFIRMED:** IEC ranking A > D > B = C stable across refutation rates. ✓
**P4 REFUTED (rate-dependent):** At p_refute=0.035, F_contested IEC = 0.00 (all nodes
refuted before accumulating citations). At p_refute=0.005, F_contested IEC = 20.00,
far exceeding B_propagation IEC = 1.00 — so the prediction is correct in the low-refutation
regime but false in the standard regime. ✗ (conditional)

### Key findings

**F_contested reveals an IEC blind spot.** At moderate refutation rates, the contested
population's nodes are refuted faster than they accumulate downstream citations. IEC
measures independent origins of *surviving* propagation chains — it cannot credit
Popperian hardiness (failed refutation attempts). A claim with 20 independent origins
but all-refuted nodes is indistinguishable from a claim with no origins at all by the
IEC metric.

This is not a defect in the Popperian hardiness concept — it is a gap in IEC. The two
properties are orthogonal: IEC measures provenance diversity, Popperian hardiness measures
test resistance. A genuinely strong claim needs high values on *both* axes. The simulation
demonstrates that tracking them separately matters.

**C_cartel_false** is the most troubling population: ELF = 4.43 even at p_refute=0.10
(refuted_fraction = 0.87). Cartel suppression keeps approximately 13% of false nodes
unrefuted even at high refutation pressure, sustaining the laundering effect. IEC
correctly identifies this as a single-origin claim (IEC=1.00), but RRW cannot detect
the suppression.

## SIM-LAUNDER-02B — Observer Epistemics: Binary Jury Collapse

**Research question:** Does forcing contextual or subjective disagreement through a binary
jury destroy the structural information that a context-preserving graph could retain?

**Design:** 4 claim types (OBJECTIVE, CONTESTED, CONTEXTUAL, SUBJECTIVE) × 10,000 jury trials.
Panel = 7 observers drawn randomly from N=100. Context-separation measured over first 30
observers per trial.

### Jury verdict distributions

| Claim type | P(jury=True) | Verdict entropy H | Mean vote fraction |
|---|---|---|---|
| OBJECTIVE | 0.9993 | 0.0083 | 0.9325 |
| CONTESTED | 0.9766 | 0.1601 | 0.8185 |
| CONTEXTUAL | 0.4869 | **0.9995** | 0.4948 |
| SUBJECTIVE | 0.4987 | **1.0000** | 0.4986 |

### Context-separation metric (within-context agreement − between-context agreement)

All four claim types returned ctx_sep = 0.0000.

**Measurement confound (critical):** The `context_separation()` function was called with
`n_obs_used=30`, slicing the *first* 30 observers. For CONTEXTUAL and SUBJECTIVE claims,
observers 0–49 are assigned context_id=1 and observers 50–99 are context_id=2. Slicing
[:30] returns only context_id=1 observers — a single-context sample — so the function
correctly returns 0.0 (only one context present). This is a **measurement bug**, not a
genuine finding about graph structure. The P2 result is invalid and the experiment must
be rerun with either `n_obs_used=100` or random observer sampling before any conclusion
can be drawn about context-preserving graph metrics.

### Popperian profile comparison

| History | n_support | n_refute_attempts | n_survived | popperian_weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low (no refutation attempts) | 40 | 0 | 0 | 40.0 |
| High (20 failed refutations) | 40 | 20 | 17 | **48.5** |

p_survive = 0.85. Each survived refutation contributes +0.5 epistemic credit.

### Prediction verdicts

**P1 REFUTED (informative inversion):** CONTEXTUAL entropy = 0.9995, CONTESTED entropy
= 0.1601, diff = 0.8394 — far above the 0.05 threshold for "indistinguishable." The binary
jury IS sensitive to contextual disagreement, but not via the expected mechanism. ✗

**P2 REFUTED (measurement confound):** ctx_sep = 0.0000 for CONTEXTUAL due to observer
slicing bug. Cannot draw conclusions. Requires rerun. ✗ (invalid)

**P3 CONFIRMED:** SUBJECTIVE entropy = 1.0000, CONTEXTUAL entropy = 0.9995, diff = 0.0005.
The binary jury cannot distinguish contextual disagreement from subjective preference — both
produce near-maximum entropy verdicts. ✓

**P4 CONFIRMED:** Popperian weight 48.5 vs 40.0 — refutation hardiness adds measurable
epistemic credit independent of raw support count. ✓

### Key findings

**P1 inversion is the most important result.** The prediction was that CONTEXTUAL and
CONTESTED would have *similar* jury entropy (the jury cannot see the contextual structure).
The actual result is the opposite: CONTEXTUAL (H≈1.0) and CONTESTED (H≈0.16) are the
*most* separable claim types by jury entropy. The jury stumbles into a coin-flip verdict for
CONTEXTUAL because the observer population is exactly split across contexts — not because
it recognizes the contextual structure.

This produces an unexpected diagnostic: a binary jury that consistently returns near-50/50
verdicts is a signal that something interesting is happening, even if the jury cannot explain
what. But the jury cannot distinguish between two structurally different explanations for
why it is getting 50/50: (a) the claim is genuinely context-dependent (CONTEXTUAL), or
(b) the evaluators have irreducibly different value systems (SUBJECTIVE). P3 confirms these
are indistinguishable by jury outcome alone.

**P2 requires a corrected rerun.** The claim that "a context-preserving graph metric can
distinguish CONTEXTUAL from CONTESTED" is untested, not refuted. Within_ctx for CONTEXTUAL
is 0.921 — correctly high — but the between-context comparison was never computed. This is
the experiment Astra called "the result I'd consider potentially more significant than the
cartel findings." It should be rerun before Astra's fifth-round feedback.

## SIM-LAUNDER-02C — Popular Falsehood vs Contested Truth

**Research question:** Does `raw_reuse_weight` rank a Popular Falsehood above a Contested
Truth with 30× more independent evidence? Does IEC correctly invert this ranking?

**Design:** 2 populations (Popular Falsehood, Contested Truth) × 3 refutation rates × 500
runs × 90 epochs per run. Also: 1 Underspecified Proposition run with context_id tracking.

**Population parameters:**
- **Popular Falsehood:** 1 false origin; high citation rate; cartel suppresses 70% of
  refutation attempts. Designed to survive through social propagation, not epistemic quality.
- **Contested Truth:** 30 independent origins; 55% support / 45% dissent; high refutation
  attempts with 60% survival rate (Popperian hardiness). Designed to survive through test
  resistance, not social consensus.

### Metric comparison by refutation rate

| p_refute | Population | RRW | IEC | ELF | Refuted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.005 | Popular Falsehood | 13.60 | 1.00 | 13.60 | 0.128 |
| 0.005 | Contested Truth | 6.86 | 30.00 | 0.23 | 0.380 |
| 0.010 | Popular Falsehood | 13.50 | 1.00 | 13.50 | 0.222 |
| 0.010 | Contested Truth | 7.13 | 30.00 | 0.24 | 0.666 |
| 0.035 | Popular Falsehood | 13.66 | 1.00 | 13.66 | 0.628 |
| 0.035 | Contested Truth | 7.80 | 30.00 | 0.26 | 0.980 |

### ELF ratio: Popular Falsehood vs Contested Truth

| p_refute | ELF(PF) | ELF(CT) | ELF ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.005 | 13.60 | 0.23 | **59.1×** |
| 0.010 | 13.50 | 0.24 | **56.9×** |
| 0.035 | 13.66 | 0.26 | **52.5×** |

### Underspecified proposition ("X is safe")

| Context | IEC | RRW | Nodes |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 — "X is safe" (3 independent roots) | 3 | 5.0 | 6 |
| C2 — "X is unsafe" (2 independent roots) | 2 | 7.0 | 8 |

Context detectable: **True**

RRW ranks C2(unsafe) > C1(safe) despite C1 having more independent evidence (IEC=3 vs 2).
Context_id tracking reveals the split and enables correct attribution.

### Prediction verdicts

**P1 CONFIRMED:** RRW ranks PF > CT at all three refutation rates (13.66 vs 7.80 at standard
rate). ✓
**P2 CONFIRMED:** IEC ranks CT > PF (30.00 vs 1.00) at all three rates. ✓
**P3 CONFIRMED:** ELF ratio 52.5× at p_refute=0.035, far above the 10× threshold. ✓
**P4 CONFIRMED:** P(PF not refuted at p_refute=0.005) = 0.872. Cartel suppression sustains
the Popular Falsehood with 87% survival even at low base refutation pressure. ✓

### Key findings

**52× ELF gap is the headline number.** The Popular Falsehood and Contested Truth have
nearly identical raw citation mass at moderate refutation rates (RRW ≈ 13.7 vs 7.8) but
differ by 52× in laundering per unit evidence. Under `raw_reuse_weight` alone, a system
evaluating these claims ranks the falsehood meaningfully higher; under IEC, the truth
ranks 30× higher. This is the adversarially stark version of the LAUNDER-01 finding
(B > A by RRW): the gap widens from ~4% to 75% in RRW terms when the comparison is
constructed as Popular Falsehood vs Contested Truth rather than generic propagation vs
corroboration.

**Cartel suppression is durable.** At p_refute=0.005, 87.2% of PF runs survive to epoch 90.
The cartel's 70% refutation suppression rate is the dominant factor — a claim that deflects
most refutation attempts can accumulate a large citation footprint while retaining low
refuted_fraction. This is the attack path that IEC alone does not address (IEC measures
origin diversity, not suppression-adjusted refutation rate).

**Underspecified proposition: RRW metric inversion.** Even when two context-disaggregated
sub-claims have different independent evidence counts (3 vs 2), RRW ranks the sub-claim
with MORE citations (C2/unsafe, 8 nodes, RRW=7.0) above the one with MORE independent
evidence (C1/safe, 6 nodes, RRW=5.0). Context_id tracking correctly identifies the split
and enables separate analysis. This replicates the core LAUNDER-02B finding (contextual
structure is not preserved by citation-count metrics) in a graph propagation setting.

## Cross-SIM Synthesis: LAUNDER Series (01 → 02A → 02B → 02C)

### RRW miscalibration ladder

Four experiments now quantify the same fundamental gap at increasing adversarial severity:

| Experiment | Comparison | RRW gap | Epistemic reality |
|---|---|---|---|
| LAUNDER-01 | B_propagation vs A_corroboration | +4% (B > A) | A has 10× more independent origins |
| LAUNDER-02A | B_propagation vs A_corroboration (ELF) | LG = 10× | Stable across refutation rates |
| LAUNDER-02C (standard) | Popular Falsehood vs Contested Truth | +75% (PF > CT) | CT has 30× more independent origins |
| LAUNDER-02C (ELF ratio) | ELF(PF) / ELF(CT) | **52–59×** | Maximum adversarial gap |

The severity scales with adversarial intent: an opportunistic propagation chain is mildly
misfavored; a deliberately suppressed falsehood with cartel protection is 59× over-credited
per unit of genuine evidence.

### Two-dimensional epistemic profile

These experiments reveal that a single metric cannot fully characterize epistemic quality.
The appropriate profile is at minimum two-dimensional:

| Dimension | Metric | What it measures | Blind spot |
|---|---|---|---|
| Provenance diversity | IEC | Number of independent origins | Doesn't credit survived tests |
| Test resistance | Refutation hardiness | Failed refutation attempts survived | Doesn't track provenance structure |

**These axes are orthogonal.** A_corroboration (high IEC, low refutation) and F_contested
(high attempted-refutation survival, fragile IEC) are differently strong claims. A claim
that scores high on both is the epistemically strongest category — and the hardest to
attack by either laundering or cartel suppression.

### What binary juries know (and don't know)

From LAUNDER-02B:

| Claim type | Jury outcome | What jury can infer | What jury cannot infer |
|---|---|---|---|
| OBJECTIVE | P≈1.0, H≈0 | "Claim is well-supported" | — |
| CONTESTED | P≈0.98, H≈0.16 | "Claim is mostly supported but with some dissent" | Whether dissent is error or evidence |
| CONTEXTUAL | P≈0.49, H≈1.0 | "Something is contentious" | Whether contention is context-dependent or value-dependent |
| SUBJECTIVE | P≈0.50, H≈1.0 | "Something is contentious" | Indistinguishable from CONTEXTUAL |

The jury is *more* sensitive to contextual disagreement than predicted (P1 inverted) — it
correctly produces a 50/50 split when the observer population is evenly split across contexts.
But this sensitivity is superficial: the jury cannot attribute the 50/50 to context structure
vs value disagreement (P3 confirmed). The graph-level measurement to verify whether structural
preservation can make this distinction remains untested (P2 confound).

### Architectural recommendation

The LAUNDER series as a whole provides empirical support for replacing `raw_reuse_weight`
with a minimum three-vector epistemic score:

```
epistemic_score = (IEC, RRW/IEC, refutation_credit)
              = (independent evidence count,
                 laundering factor [ELF],
                 survived refutation attempts × discount)
```

At minimum, adding IEC as a secondary ranking signal would correct the 10–59× calibration
error. Adding refutation_credit addresses the Popperian dimension that IEC cannot capture.
The specific CDL design (weights, aggregation function, scoring epoch) is a governance
question — but the empirical case for three-dimensional scoring is now established across
four simulation experiments.
