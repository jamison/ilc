# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

ILC is an evidence-first epistemic protocol. We expect contributors to bring that same commitment to the community: engage in good faith, support claims with evidence, and accept refutation graciously.

**Expected behavior:**
- Be direct and honest. Vague claims that cannot be evaluated are not useful.
- Distinguish between "I believe X" and "the protocol specifies X" and "the data shows X."
- Accept that you might be wrong. Refutation is a feature of the protocol and of the community.
- Treat other contributors with basic respect, especially when you disagree.
- Give credit where it is due.

**Not acceptable:**
- Personal attacks, harassment, or discrimination on the basis of identity
- Deliberately misleading claims about protocol behavior or security
- Spam, solicitation, or off-topic promotion
- Disclosing private information about contributors without consent
- Using this project's community channels to organize or promote unrelated activities

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions (if enabled), and any official ILC communication channels.

---

## Enforcement

Reports of unacceptable behavior can be sent to `ilcops@proton.me`. All reports will be reviewed. Enforcement actions range from a warning to a permanent ban depending on severity and recurrence.

---

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is written for ILC's specific context: an early-stage open-source protocol with a small community. It may be revised as the project grows.
