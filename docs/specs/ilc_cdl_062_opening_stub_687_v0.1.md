## 1. What this stub does and does not do

This stub opens CDL-062 as a constitutional lane for sovereign-substrate
research.

It does:
- authorize the project to begin disciplined candidate research
- carry forward the Phase 662 opening-admissibility gates as active constraints
- define what the CDL-062 lane is allowed to produce
- set the non-ratification boundary so the window does not collapse research
  into final selection

It does not:
- ratify CDL-062
- select a final substrate
- authorize production deployment of any candidate
- reopen or relax rows 5, 6, 7, 8, or 9

---

## 2. What CDL-062 is

CDL-062 is the sovereign-substrate research and selection constitutional lane.

Its purpose is to govern:
- what substrate families are admissible for carrying ILC epoch-boundary state
- what evidence standard must be met before a substrate family becomes a
  finalist
- what proof obligations a finalist must satisfy before final Option-B selection
  is authorized

CDL-062 will be ratified only when:
- the research lane has produced a narrowed survivor set with honest evidence
- the project is ready to authorize final Option-B implementation preparation
- explicit human authorization for that selection has been given

None of those conditions are met in this window. This is the opening, not the
close.
