## 1. Preamble

This Contributor License Agreement ("CLA") records the terms under which the
ILC project accepts Contributions for inclusion in the Project. It is recorded
as Genesis-authority governance policy for the ILC repository and public-source
allowlist scope.

This CLA is not external legal opinion, does not constitute legal advice, and
does not claim review, approval, or enforceability by external counsel or any
third-party authority. Contributors with legal questions should consult their
own attorney before submitting Contributions.

## 2. Definitions

**Contribution** means any source code, documentation, data, specification,
test, configuration, build script, or other material submitted by You for
inclusion in the Project.

**You** means the individual or legal entity submitting a Contribution.

**Project** means the ILC project and repository, including the public-source
allowlist scope defined by project governance.

## 3. Copyright License Grant

You grant to the Project, and to recipients of software or materials distributed
by the Project, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free,
irrevocable copyright license to reproduce, prepare derivative works of,
publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense, and distribute Your
Contributions and derivative works in source or object form.

This CLA v1 uses a license-grant model rather than a copyright-assignment
model. A later copyright-assignment model, if ever adopted, requires separate
governance authorization and external counsel review or disposition before it is
treated as project policy.

## 4. Patent Grant

You grant to the Project, and to recipients of software distributed by the
Project, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable patent
license to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and otherwise
transfer the Contribution.

This patent license applies only to patent claims licensable by You that are
necessarily infringed by Your Contribution alone or by combination of Your
Contribution with the Project to which it was submitted.

## 5. Distribution Terms

You agree that the Project may distribute Your Contributions under the
AGPL-3.0-only license and any compatible project-governed distribution terms
later authorized for the public-source allowlist scope.

## 6. Public-Source Allowlist Scope

This CLA covers Contributions submitted for files included in the public-source
allowlist established by Phase 1333 and audited by Phase 1443. The public-source
allowlist is the scope of external contribution accepted under this CLA unless a
later governance action expands the scope.

Files marked internal-only, investigative, diagnostic, scaffolding,
private-process, or otherwise excluded from public RC release remain outside the
accepted external contribution scope until a later explicit authorization
removes or supersedes that exclusion.

## 7. Contributor Acknowledgments

By submitting a Contribution, You represent that:

1. You are legally entitled to grant the rights described in this CLA.
2. Each Contribution is Your original work, or You have sufficient rights to
   submit it under this CLA.
3. You have not included third-party material without appropriate rights.
4. You understand that accepted Contributions may be distributed under
   AGPL-3.0-only terms or later project-governed distribution terms.

## 8. Governance-Authority Declaration

This CLA is a Genesis-authority internal governance policy document. It records
the terms under which the ILC project accepts contributions as a matter of
internal project governance. It is not external legal opinion, does not
constitute legal advice, and has not been reviewed or approved by external
legal counsel. Contributors with legal questions should consult their own
attorney.

## 9. Acceptance

Contributions are accepted subject to this CLA. A contributor's submission of a
Contribution to the Project constitutes agreement to these terms. Where
practical, contributors should record explicit acknowledgment, such as a signed
acceptance record or a `Signed-off-by` line in commits.

## 10. Gap 7 Non-Closure and Carry-Forward

Phase 1444 completes the CLA text milestone for Gap 7 only. It does not fully
close Gap 7.

The US provisional patent application remains deferred to external action. The
trademark registration remains deferred to external action. Those items must be
carried forward by Phase 1445 or later planning and must not be inferred as
complete from this CLA.

## 11. Non-Activation Boundaries

This CLA does not publish the public RC, activate any runtime flag, open,
ratify, or mutate any CDL, perform ECU or ILC operations, authorize public
repository publication, authorize package publication, authorize release
artifact signing, or trigger an epoch transition.
