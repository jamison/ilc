## 2. Locked row-7 rule

`row_7_closed_as_criteria_lock_with_future_proof_obligations`

Locked row-7 rule:
- censorship against public legitimacy surfaces includes practical exclusion,
  not only packet-level denial
- one human operator, one dashboard, or one provider-specific shell may not be
  the ordinary public legitimacy surface
- later substrate candidates must satisfy the strong exitability and
  replayability threshold before claiming row-7 compliance

The proof obligations from Phase 674 are now part of the row-7 lock.

## 3. Locked row-8 rule

`row_8_closed_as_independence_and_exclusion_criteria_lock`

Locked row-8 rule:
- later substrate families may not make protocol legitimacy subordinate to an
  outside veto authority
- outside systems may carry or settle already-legitimate protocol state, but
  may not become the constitutional center that authors legitimacy
- the Phase-673 exclusion matrix is binding for later substrate admissibility

## 4. Genesis bootstrap exception treatment

Genesis remains:
- the canonical legitimacy lineage root
- a bounded bootstrap necessity
- extraordinary authority only where separately constitutionalized

Genesis does not become:
- a general anti-bottleneck exemption
- an excuse for indefinite founder sovereignty
- a reusable justification for later third-party chokepoints

## 5. Hard carry-forward obligations

`future_substrate_must_satisfy_row_7_proof_obligations_before_row_7_compliance_claim`

Carry-forward obligations for later substrate lanes:
- every candidate must be tested against the row-7 proof obligations
- every candidate must be classified against the row-8 exclusion matrix
- no candidate may be treated as compliant merely because it is popular,
  performant, or easy to operate
- no later lane may reopen row 7 or row 8 by implication or vendor preference

## 6. What this lock does not decide

This lock does not decide:
- the final Option-B substrate winner
- row-5 privacy-preserving public legitimacy mechanism
- `CDL-062` opening
- runtime implementation details for any substrate candidate
