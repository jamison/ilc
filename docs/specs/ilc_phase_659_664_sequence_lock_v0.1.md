## 2. Practical row-6 closure target

The practical row-6 target is not "final metaphysical truth." The target is a
durable governance rule for what a later backend may carry forward and what it
may never author or override.

`backend_means_later_sovereign_settlement_substrate_family`
`protocol_legitimacy_surfaces_remain_upstream_of_backend_choice`

Working practical definitions:
- upstream means the source of canonical legitimacy or authority
- downstream means a later backend may record, order, anchor, finalize, or
  settle what the protocol already made legitimate, but it may not create that
  legitimacy on its own
- backend means a later sovereign settlement substrate family that could be
  opened later by `CDL-062`, such as a custom minimal L1, an external rollup or
  L2, or another later chain-backed settlement substrate

The legitimacy surfaces that must remain upstream of later backend choice are:
- public admission and identity activation
- canonical namespace and handle authority
- quorum, panel, and evaluation authority
- public settlement legitimacy
- public reputation continuity

The row-6 closure question is therefore practical:
- can a later backend author public legitimacy, or only carry and settle it
- can a later backend override protocol truth, or only anchor protocol truth
  that was already made legitimate upstream
- can a forked or alternate backend inherit canonical identity, namespace,
  quorum authority, settlement legitimacy, or reputation without protocol-side
  lineage and receipts
