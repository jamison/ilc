# ILC Rights, Licenses, and Gated-Access Surfaces Disposition 729 v0.1

**Phase:** 729  
**Window:** 727-732  
**Date:** 2026-04-18  
**Author:** Codex

`rights_licensing_not_collapsed_into_refutation_criterion`

## 1. Baseline

Window `727-732` is active under the Phase `727` sequence lock and the Phase
`728` carry-forward selection. The selected lane is adjacent gated-economy
hardening, not financial-shard activation and not `CDL-062` convergence.

The immediate purpose of this phase is to turn the existing rights/licensing
memo into a bounded disposition that reduces implementation drift without
pretending a new rights contract is already constitutionally ratified.

## 2. Rights and licensing treatment

Rights, licensing, attribution conditions, and access conditions are not the
same thing as epistemic refutation.

They are better treated as:

- metadata,
- provenance,
- contract surfaces,
- and dispute/adjudication surfaces.

This means a license breach, attribution dispute, or access-condition dispute
is not modeled as if it were merely a scientific contradiction.

`rights_dispute_surface_not_yet_ratified`

Panels, evidence, and review records may still matter in a dispute, but the
thing being adjudicated is not automatically the same object as
`refutation_criterion`.

## 3. Layer assignment

The correct layered split is:

- **L1 / public anchor layer**
  - creator identity / attribution anchor,
  - public node or public root reference,
  - rights/licensing metadata,
  - public promotion lineage,
  - visibility and existence anchors for gated/private spaces.
- **Gated/private shard contract layer**
  - minimal header surface,
  - access-model declaration,
  - fee-hook or subscription/contract reference hooks,
  - private/public boundary commitments,
  - lineage continuity for private-to-public promotion.
- **L2/L3 business-logic layer**
  - recurring billing logic,
  - media delivery or download control,
  - enterprise access policies,
  - dynamic pricing or auction logic,
  - richer market microstructure and customer workflows.

`gated_access_layer_assignment_locked_for_727_732`
`l1_l2_l3_boundary_for_gated_access_recorded`

What remains at the public anchor layer is enough to preserve existence,
identity linkage, continuity, and auditable downstream publication. What
belongs in gated/private shard contracts is the minimum surface needed to point
to access rights and continuity without exposing private contents by default.

## 4. Dispute and adjudication posture

The dispute posture in this phase is:

- false attribution,
- removed attribution,
- false claim of exclusive rights,
- and breach of a declared usage condition

should be treated as disputes over declared conditions, provenance, and
contract surfaces rather than automatically as epistemic falsification.

This does not prevent evidence review or panel involvement. It means the repo
must not silently reuse the epistemic-refutation machinery as a substitute for
a dedicated rights/dispute contract before such a contract has been explicitly
defined and accepted.

This phase therefore records the boundary but does not ratify a new rights
contract.

## 5. Deferred implementation surfaces

The following remain explicitly deferred:

- a dedicated rights-dispute contract,
- automatic downstream usage-condition enforcement,
- full rights/licensing semantics at L1,
- runtime capability-token implementation,
- recurring-payment business logic,
- enterprise access management,
- dynamic pricing and other market overlays.

These surfaces remain future hardening or implementation work rather than
in-window constitutional closure.

## 6. Non-goals

This phase does not include:

- decision-log mutation,
- any CDL ratification,
- financial-shard activation,
- runtime implementation of subscriptions, delivery, or enterprise access,
- any mutation of `ilc_core/` or `ilc_consensus/`.

This phase does not claim a new ratified rights contract. It records the
architectural disposition only.
