## 5. Boundary and anti-circumvention semantics

Genesis intervention is not a general parallel appeal path and may not be used to bypass CDL-V4 ordinary reopening.

Genesis intervention may be invoked only for capture, constitutional violation, or time-critical emergency where the ordinary reopening process is unavailable, compromised, or too slow to prevent irreversible harm.

Any CDL-V6 invocation requires mandatory post hoc CDL-V4 review once ordinary governance is available.

This phase ratifies CDL-V6 together with CDL-V4 to close the coupled governance boundary rather than leaving a governance gap.
