## 5. Boundary and escalation semantics

`CDL-V4` applies retroactively to previously ratified CDL decisions with no blanket statute of limitations.

Reopening remains ordinary governance unless capture, constitutional violation, or time-critical emergency makes the ordinary reopening process unavailable or too slow to prevent irreversible harm.

Genesis escalation is justified only for capture, constitutional violation, or time-critical emergency where the ordinary reopening process is unavailable or too slow to prevent irreversible harm.

Any reopened decision that returns to ratification must satisfy the currently ratified CDL-V3 diversity requirement.

This phase ratifies CDL-V4 together with CDL-V6 to close the coupled governance boundary rather than leaving a governance gap.
