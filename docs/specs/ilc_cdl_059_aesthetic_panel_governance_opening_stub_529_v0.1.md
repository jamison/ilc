## 1. Lane identity

CDL-059 opens a constitutional lane for aesthetic panel governance over Register 2 expressive
content. `cdl_059_governs_aesthetic_panel_governance`

## 2. Problem statement

ADR-0023 identified a missing constitutional lane for transparent, informational-only
quality signaling over expressive content. The lane must avoid truth-claim governance overlap
and must not create blocking authority.

## 3. Candidate options

Options considered:
- retain ADR-0023 as research guidance only
- flat equal-weight aesthetic panel
- diversity-maximizing aesthetic panel

## 4. Selected option

`selected_option: diversity_maximizing_aesthetic_panel`

The selected option is a diversity-maximizing aesthetic panel for Register 2 expressive/creative
content. The lane is `layer_2_informational_only` and does not create blocking authority.
