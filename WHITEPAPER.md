# ILC Whitepapers

ILC's technical and economic design is documented in two companion papers. These are working drafts — the protocol is actively being built and some implementation details have moved ahead of the paper text. CDLs and ADRs are the authoritative record for ratified protocol decisions.

---

## Technical Paper — Protocol Architecture

**[ILC Technical Paper (Draft v0.2)](docs/ILC_Technical_Paper_Draft_v0.2.md)**
*The Epistemological Graph as a Compute Substrate for Agentic Economies*

Covers: morphogenetic hypergraph architecture, PoIL (Proof of Intelligent Labor), seven truth primitives, Merkle-Laplacian dual commitment, VRF jury assignment, object-sharded DAG consensus, and the post-quantum identity stack.

---

## Economic Paper — ECU and Settlement Design

**[ILC Economic Paper (Draft v0.2)](docs/ILC_Economic_Paper_Draft_v0.2.md)**
*Economics After Scarcity: Verified Work, Refutation Markets, and Epistemic Credit*

Covers: ECU (Epistemic Compute Unit) mechanics, Werner productive-credit model (*W_e = ΔH / E_cost*), temporal decay and mandatory conversion, anti-hoarding design, ILC settlement token supply cap (25,920,000 — one Platonic Year × 1,000), and the economics of agentic labor.
